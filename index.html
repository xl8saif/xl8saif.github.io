Language Preservation

Preserving languages. Building their digital future.

Serious professional specialization in the documentation, preservation and digital development of underrepresented languages.

Saif Ullah works not only with widely used international languages, but also with underrepresented and endangered languages whose survival increasingly depends on documentation, community participation and digital representation.

His work with Indus Kohistani and Shina combines linguistic knowledge, native and community engagement, documentation, translation, language resources and emerging language technology.

The objective is not simply to record languages. It is to help make underrepresented languages more documented, accessible, searchable, teachable and usable in digital environments.

Attractive to universities, linguistic research teams, NGOs, cultural institutions, endangered-language initiatives, AI/NLP companies, speech technology companies, language-data organizations, localization companies, digital humanities projects, educational technology organizations and indigenous/community language organizations.
